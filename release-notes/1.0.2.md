Beanbook 1.0.2 reworks the document scanner and fixes a long list of smaller problems.

**Scanner.** A new review screen with a thumbnail strip, Retake and Add page, a crop screen with a magnifier and edge handles, better page detection, flat black-and-white and greyscale styles with an Automatic default, A4 or Letter page size, and a choice of photo size.

**Also new.** Accounts can carry details such as the bank and be closed with a date, scheduled backups can be encrypted, deleted documents are kept for 30 days in a trash on the Organize tab, and buttons look the same everywhere.

**Safer.** Fixes for a remote retry that could send an entry twice, backups written from files that changed, and a PDF page that crashed the viewer.
