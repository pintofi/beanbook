Beanbook 1.0.3 gives the scanner a zoom slider, gives Home, Journal and Balances one shared Filters button, and fixes a number of smaller problems.

**Scanner.** The zoom is a slider above the shutter, with guides on each side. Slide the number right to zoom in and left to zoom out; let go and it springs back to the middle, and the zoom stays where it is.

**Everywhere else.** Home, Journal and Balances share one Filters button for the period, the search, and tags or links. The Journal now has the Units, At cost and Market value choice, and the same choice is on the widgets themselves. Reports say so when the phone's System WebView is too old to show Fava's pages, instead of staying blank.

**Fixed.** Unlinking a document no longer rewrites every line ending in a ledger saved with Windows line endings. An archive whose names go up and down a folder is read correctly, and nothing could ever be written outside the project. The Units, At cost and Market value choice no longer breaks a word at large text sizes, and its buttons on a widget no longer flicker while the widget is resized. Setup, deleting the open project, opening a folder and a script's live output also got fixes.
