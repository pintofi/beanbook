Beanbook 1.0.4 changes how an entry's flag is chosen, lets a backup be restored from the first-run setup, makes the widgets follow the system colours, and fixes a number of problems found in daily use.

**Entry form.** The flag is one choice of Cleared, Pending or Other. Other takes any flag Beancount allows, such as `P`, and says what is wrong with one it would refuse. An entry that carries such a flag can now be edited, saved and cloned from the form, which it could not before. Account suggestions follow Fava's ranking, the suggestion list floats over the fields below it, and templates are one list where each can be renamed or deleted.

**Journal and Accounts.** The Errors, Pending and Other flags filters are one choice. The Accounts page has a search box and the currencies each account allows.

**Backups, Mirror and widgets.** A Beanbook backup (.bcaz) can be restored from the first-run setup and from the Projects page, and comes back as a new project. In Mirror, a file deleted on one side can now be settled. The widgets take their colours from the system palette when dynamic colour is on, and the Balances widget shows the basis you tap.

**Fixed.** Market values worked out through a price quoted the other way round now round as Beancount and Fava do. The blank-posting sentence on the form and the Accounts page follow the ledger's own rounding and separators, and Use last no longer fills in the currency of the posting left blank. A tap on a notification opens the page it is about.
