Beanbook keeps your books where they already live: in ordinary Beancount text files, on your phone. It runs the real Beancount and Fava on the device, so the figures you see are the ones bean-check and Fava produce on a desktop.

There is no account to create, no sign-in and no server run by this project. Your ledger is a folder of text files you own.

<b>What you can do</b>
• Read your ledger: a Home page with net worth and where the money went, a searchable Journal, and Balances for every account.
• Show figures three ways, as Fava does: Units, At cost, or Market value.
• Add entries and other Beancount directives with a form that shows the exact text before it is written.
• Edit the files themselves, with every save re-read by Beancount before it is called saved, and an Undo for the last change.
• Scan receipts and statements into PDFs and file them under an account.
• Open Fava's Reports inside the app.
• Mirror a project to a folder you choose, and back it up with an optional passphrase.
• Use Beancount plugins and Fava extensions, and run your own Python scripts (Expert edition).
• Home-screen widgets for balances and net worth.

<b>Your data stays yours</b>
• No telemetry, analytics, crash uploads or ads.
• Local mode makes no network requests.
• If you turn on Share on Wi-Fi, other devices can reach your Reports only while it is on, only on a private network, and only after signing in.
• Nothing is sold, and there is nothing to subscribe to.

<b>Two editions</b>
The Expert edition adds plugins and packages that include native code, fetched only when you ask. The Play edition does without them. Both are free.
