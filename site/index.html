Beanbook:Android

Your money, written down where you can read it.

Record what you spend and earn, see where it goes, and keep receipts with the entries they belong to. Everything stays on your phone as ordinary text files you can open on a computer. No account to make, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Your file stays yours

Your records are text files in a folder: your own layout, comments and receipts kept as they are, not locked inside a database only this app can read.

  • Edit without giving up text

    Use guided entry for transactions and common directives, or edit every included file directly with highlighting, search, validation, and stale-write detection.

    Entries and editing

  • Read the full reports

    Use Fava reports, charts, account views, and BQL queries from the device.

    Reports

  • Move data manually, free

    Import, export, backup, and restore, like everything else, are free in both editions.

    Everything is free

  • Keep control of network use

    Local mode makes zero network requests. Remote mode connects only to the Fava server address you enter.
