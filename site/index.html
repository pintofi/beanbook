Beancount for Android

Plain-text double-entry accounting, with the real Beancount and Fava running on your phone.

Overview Privacy Contribution keys

Your books are ordinary Beancount text files in a folder you choose. The app embeds CPython and runs upstream Beancount and Fava — not a re-implementation of them — so what you see on your phone is what bean-check and Fava produce on your desktop.

Your files stay yours

Nothing is uploaded. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours anywhere in the picture. If you uninstall the app tomorrow, your ledger is still a folder of text files you can open in any editor. See the privacy policy.

What it does

  • Write and edit entries — transactions, balances, prices, every directive Beancount supports, with the raw file editable by hand when you prefer.
  • Read your accounts — a journal and an account balances list, computed by Beancount itself.
  • Full Fava reports — balance sheet, income statement, trial balance, holdings, charts, and BQL queries.
  • Attach receipts — photograph them, crop them, and file them against a transaction as linked documents.
  • Import, export, back up and restore — always available, in every edition, with no limits.
  • Connect to your own Fava server, if you already host one.

Two editions

The two editions have the same features, including plugins and Fava extensions. They differ only where Google Play's policies require it:

EditionWhereDifference
Play Google Play Runs pure-Python plugins only, and never downloads code. Updates through Play.
Expert Direct download Can install packages containing native code, and can update itself from a signed package.

Neither edition contains a billing SDK, a purchase flow, or advertising.

Free, and paid

Every install starts with a 30-day trial with everything unlocked. No account, no card, no network call — the app counts the days on your device.

When the trial ends, nothing is taken away except the paid features. Adding, editing and deleting entries keeps working. The journal and balances keep working. Editing the raw files keeps working. Import, export, backup and restore are never gated, during the trial or after it — getting your data out is not something you should have to pay for. The contribution keys page has the details.
