Beanbook:Support

Beanbook is free. All of it.

No trial, no paid features, no keys and no ads, in either edition. If the app is useful to you, you can help it along with a donation.

Tips go to Ko-fi.

If Beanbook has been useful, you can leave a tip on ko-fi.com/pintofi, in your browser. It unlocks nothing: everything is already yours.

Open ko-fi.com/pintofi

The Expert edition links to it from About. The Play edition does not, because Google Play's payment rules do not allow an app to point at a payment page outside Play.

Other ways to help.

Report a bug, suggest something, or send a patch. The app is free software, licensed GPL-2.0-only, and the source is public.
