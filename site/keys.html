What is always free

Every accounting operation. Adding, editing, deleting and duplicating any directive; the journal; the account balances list; editing the raw files by hand with highlighting, search and validation; multiple projects; safe mode and diagnostics.

And all of your data, in and out. Manual import, export, backup creation and restore are free in every edition, forever, with no limits. Getting your records out of an accounting app is not a feature worth charging for, and the files are plain text you could copy by hand anyway.

The 30-day trial

Every fresh install unlocks all three tiers for 30 days. There is no account, no card and no network call — the app records the date on your device and counts down. When it ends, the paid features switch off and nothing else changes: your ledger is untouched and every file stays where it was.

Winding your device clock backwards does not extend the trial. A reinstall does start a new one — we could prevent that by fingerprinting your device, and we would rather not identify you.

The tiers

Each tier includes everything in the one before it.

The organising principle: free is fully functional but manual; paid is automated and analytical. A free user can do every accounting operation and move data in and out as often as they like — it simply takes more taps.

How to get a key

Keys are bought here, in your browser, never inside the app. After payment you receive a key by email, and you paste it into the app under More → Contribute → Enter key.

How a key works

  • It is a short block of text, signed with an Ed25519 key.
  • The app verifies that signature on your device, with no network call. There is no licence server, and no check-in.
  • It carries the tier, the email address it was issued to, and an order reference. The app shows the tier in Settings so you can tell which key is installed; the address is only there so a key can be reissued if you lose it.
  • If your trial is still running, the app uses whichever gives you more, so buying during a trial never downgrades you.
  • Upgrading between tiers is a replacement key at the price difference, issued manually.

Why it works this way

The app contains no billing code of any kind — no Play Billing, no payment SDK, nothing. It can check a key, and that is the whole of its payment-related surface. That keeps a proprietary, GPL-incompatible dependency out of a GPL-2.0-only app, and it means the app never has to phone home to decide what you are allowed to do.
