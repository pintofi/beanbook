Contribution keys

Free stays useful. Keys add analysis and automation.

A signed key is checked offline on your device. There is no account, billing SDK, licence server, or activation limit.

The 30-day trial

Every fresh install starts with all three tiers unlocked. No card, account, device fingerprint, or network call is required.

During the trial

Insight, Workflow, and Power features are available for 30 days.

After it ends

The ledger stays untouched. Writing, editing, the journal, balances, raw files, and every manual data transfer remain available.

What each tier adds

Each paid tier includes the one before it. Prices are in US dollars.

Tier Price What it adds
Free Free Adding and editing every kind of entry, the journal, account balances, the text editor, several ledgers, and all manual import, export, backup and restore.
Insight $9.99 Full Fava reports, charts, views, and BQL queries.
Workflow $14.99 Everything in Insight, plus receipts and documents, the camera and scanner, PDFs, home-screen widgets, keeping a folder in step, and scheduled backups.
Power $19.99 Everything in Workflow, plus connecting to your own Fava server, importers for bank statements, scripts, and add-ons.

Data in, data out, and recovery are never paywalled. Manual import, export, backup, and restore remain free with no count limits.

Payment links are not live yet.

No payment account has been connected to this page. It is publishable as-is, but there is currently no way to buy a key here.

When sales open, keys will be purchased in a normal browser outside the app. The app itself will continue to offer only an Enter key field.

How a contribution key works

  1. You receive signed text.The format begins with BCA1 and carries the tier, issue email, order reference, issue date, and signing-key identifier.
  2. You enter it once.The raw key is stored in app-private storage and does not appear in logs, backups, screenshots, widgets, or diagnostics.
  3. The app checks it offline.Ed25519 verification uses public keys compiled into the app. There is no licence server or network call.
  4. The better grant wins.An active trial cannot reduce the tier from a key you already entered.
Unlimited devices, soft email binding.

The email is a social deterrent, not a device lock. There is no activation counter.
