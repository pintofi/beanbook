Requirements and size

Android

Android 10 (API 29) or later. Android 9 is not supported.

Processor

arm64-v8a and x86_64. There is no 32-bit build.

Optional libraries use more space.

The Expert edition downloads extra Python libraries only when you ask for one. The app shows what it would download, and how large it is, before anything is fetched.

Which edition should you use?

Play

Use it when you want updates through the Play Store and your add-ons do not need to download libraries or use your phone's own features.

Expert

Use it when your add-ons need libraries downloaded on demand or access to your phone's own features, and you are comfortable installing signed releases yourself.

Files are identical between editions. A project created in one opens unchanged in the other and in desktop Beancount.
