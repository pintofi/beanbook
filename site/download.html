Edition comparison

Everything not listed as a difference is shared, including local Beancount and Fava, reports, editing, documents, backups, remote mode, and every other feature.

Requirements and size

10+ Android Android 10 (API 29) or later.

64-bit Processor arm64-v8a and x86_64.

Optional libraries use more space.

The Expert edition downloads extra Python libraries only when you install a plugin that needs them, or ask for one by name. When you ask by name, the app lists what it would download before anything is fetched.

Which edition should you use?

Play

Use it when you want updates through the Play Store and your plugins can come from a file and do not need downloaded libraries or your phone's own features.

Expert

Use it when you want plugins installed from the in-app list, with the libraries they need, or access to your phone's own features, and you are comfortable installing signed releases yourself.

Files are identical between editions. A project created in one opens unchanged in the other and in desktop Beancount.
